## 👋 Who we are

ASRR Technologies builds software that makes complex industries simpler — from BIM-driven
construction workflows and ERP to product configurators, 3D visualization, and the web apps
and mobile tools our clients rely on every day.

## 🚀 What we work on

- **Alpha** — our Building Sector Innovation Platform: ERP, configuration, sales, and 3D
  visualization tooling for construction companies. → [docs.alpha.asrr.nl](https://docs.alpha.asrr.nl)
- **BIM & Revit tooling** — Autodesk Revit add-ins, IFC/BIM automation, and a shared
  [Revit Core library](https://github.com/ASRRtechnologies/lib-asrr-revit-core).
- **Configurators & viewers** — real-time product/façade configurators with interactive 3D.
- **Web & mobile** — customer-facing sites and apps for clients across the Netherlands,
  Suriname, and the wider Caribbean.
